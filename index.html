Our story

From Bangkok to Canton, by way of family.

Bangkok Cafe was founded by Tamsin Ramasut — daughter of the family behind The Thai House, and a Cardiff girl through and through.

After years travelling between Thailand and Wales, learning the cuisine in her father's kitchen and across Bangkok's street corners, Tamsin returned home to bring something the city was missing: a younger, more relaxed Thai dining room with the same uncompromising kitchen.

Ten years on, we've helped redefine Cowbridge Road — and become a fixture of Cardiff's neighbourhood eating scene.
